Our story

Software that decides, not just responds.

Brain Waves started with a simple observation: most business software still waits to be told what to do, one click at a time. We build the other kind — agents that take a goal, work out the steps, and carry them through, so people can spend their time on judgment calls instead of repetitive execution.

We're registered as a Micro Enterprise under India's MSME framework and operate out of Palladam, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, working across agentic AI, automation, and custom intelligent systems.

What we believe

The principles behind every agent we ship.

Reason before action

An agent that acts without understanding the goal is just automation with extra steps. We build the reasoning layer first.

No unsupported claims

We describe what a system does, not what it might one day do. Scope stays honest, in our pitch and in the product.

Built to be understood

Every workflow we hand over is documented and explainable — not a black box you have to trust blindly.
